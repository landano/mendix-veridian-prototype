## Notes on Stakeholder Selection

The acceptance criteria requires feedback confirming **viability** and **usability**. Aim for at least two of:

- A technical stakeholder from the Veridian / Cardano Foundation team (validates protocol correctness)
- A domain stakeholder from land administration (validates real-world applicability)
- A Mendix platform stakeholder (validates integration approach)
- A potential end user — chief, representative, or land owner role (validates usability)
